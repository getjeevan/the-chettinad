├─ Geo-targeting: Specific localities (3-7 km radius)

├─ Offer: 20-30% discount on first order

## 👥 Phase 4: Influencer Marketing (Weeks 4-12)

### 4.1 Micro-Influencer Identification

**Target Profile:**
- Followers: 1K-10K (micro-influencer sweet spot)
- Engagement Rate: >5% (authentic audience)
- Content: Food, lifestyle, local reviews
- Audience: Your target city/neighborhood
- Authenticity: No bot followers (check with tools)

**Tools for Discovery:**
- Instagram hashtag search (#[city]foodie, #[city]biryani, etc.)
- HypeAuditor or AspireIQ
- Manual Google search: "[city] food influencer Instagram"

**Target Numbers:**
- Phase 1: 10 micro-influencers
- Phase 2: 20 micro-influencers
- Phase 3: 50+ micro-influencers (scaling)

**Campaign Type: "Honest Unboxing"**

## 💡 Phase 6: Direct Ordering System (Weeks 8-12)

### 6.1 Why Direct Ordering Matters

**Problem:** Dependency on Swiggy/Zomato (30% commission loss)  
**Solution:** Drive 15-20% of orders direct to save margin

**Financial Impact:**
```
1,000 orders/month via aggregators:
- Revenue: ₹5,00,000 (₹500 avg)
- Commission: ₹1,50,000 (30%)
- Net: ₹3,50,000

100 orders/month direct (10%):
- Revenue: ₹50,000
- Commission: ₹0
- Net: ₹50,000

Monthly margin improvement: ₹15,000 (3% extra profit)
Annual impact: ₹1.8 Lakh saved!
```

### 6.2 Implementation Options

**Option 1: WhatsApp Ordering (Fastest)**
- Setup: 2-3 days
- Cost: Free (WhatsApp Business)
- Conversion: 15-20% of engaged audience
- Tools: OrderBot, ChatBot builder

**Option 2: Website Ordering (Professional)**
- Setup: 1-2 weeks
- Cost: ₹500-1000/month (Shopify, Wix, Bubble.io)
- Conversion: 5-10% of website visitors
- SEO benefit: Yes (direct business)

**Option 3: Mobile App (Scale)**
- Setup: 4-6 weeks
- Cost: ₹50K-1L initial + ₹10K/month
- Conversion: 10-15% on app (higher ROI)
- Push notification advantage: 30-40% open rate

### 6.3 Recommended Approach

**Phase 1 (Week 8-9):** WhatsApp ordering (quick wins)  
**Phase 2 (Week 10-11):** Website ordering (professional touch)  
**Phase 3 (Week 12+):** Mobile app (scale & retention)
